1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in The Art of Dining, an Android game by Alexander Orekhov, operating as Takoda Digital. It covers the current closed-testing application, including table-setting challenges and the Classic Lemonade activity.

The application package is com.takodadigital.theartofdining. This policy was prepared against version 1.1.0 (versionCode 25). The Cooking section is still under development.

Having no registration system does not mean that no data is processed. The game stores information on your device and includes Google's advertising software, as explained below.

2. Who We Are

The developer responsible for information handled directly by us is Alexander Orekhov / Takoda Digital, based in Cyprus.

For questions or privacy requests, email takodadigital@gmail.com. The app has no user accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases.

3. Information We Collect and Process

Information kept by the game on your device
Completed levels and activities, the current level bookmark, and counters used to limit advertising frequency, including accumulated active play time and completed-level counts. These support gameplay and are not uploaded to a developer-operated game server.
Information you send us
If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, message and anything you choose to include. We use this information to reply, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.
Information processed by advertising software
The Google Mobile Ads SDK can process network and device information, identifiers, interactions and diagnostics. This is separate from local game progress and is described in section 5.

No separate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics integration is configured in the inspected application. AdMob itself includes measurement components; this does not amount to a promise that the app has no analytics-related processing.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise GPS location permissions. Advertising software may still infer an approximate location from an IP address.

4. Locally Stored Game Progress

Game progress and advertising-frequency counters are stored in the app's private Android storage using SharedPreferences. There is no developer-operated cloud save, account synchronisation or remote progress database in this version. Android app backup is disabled in the application's configuration.

You can remove this local information by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the app. This resets your progress and local counters. There is no in-app delete-account feature because there are no accounts.

We cannot remotely access or delete the progress stored only on your device. Removing local progress does not itself delete information already processed by third-party services.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob, version 25.5.0, for rewarded hints and advertising between eligible levels. The inspected closed-testing build uses Google's demo app and advert-unit identifiers, including in its release bundle. Its bundled early activities currently provide free hints; the later-level advertising eligibility conditions are not reached in that content.

Test identifiers are not a guarantee of zero processing. Google documents the following categories for this SDK:

  • IP addresses, which can indicate a general location.
  • Product interactions, such as launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostics relating to app and SDK performance.
  • Device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where available, and other applicable identifiers.

Google describes purposes including advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. The compiled app includes advertising ID and Android AdServices permissions supplied by its dependencies. Actual processing depends on SDK behaviour, device settings and service configuration. Source inspection does not establish every network operation performed by Google's software.

See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

6. Consent and Advertising Choices

Current implementation: the inspected build does not show an in-app advertising consent form or provide an in-app personalisation switch. Google's User Messaging Platform library is included transitively, but the app does not call its consent-management APIs or check consent before requesting adverts.

Choosing to watch a rewarded advert is an optional gameplay action. It is not a substitute for privacy consent where consent is required. The app does not treat the existence of this policy as consent.

Android may let you reset or delete your advertising ID or manage advertising privacy settings, depending on your device and version. These controls do not necessarily stop all SDK processing. Google also explains available controls in its privacy documentation.

Consent requirements and the advertising configuration need to be reviewed before production advertising is enabled. This policy does not claim that those requirements have already been implemented.

7. Third-Party Services

The application is distributed through Google Play and contains Google's advertising libraries. Their services are governed by their own applicable terms and privacy information, including Google's Privacy Policy. Contacting us through Gmail also involves email service providers.

This legal website is designed for GitHub Pages hosting. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts, cookies or external JavaScript dependencies to the website. GitHub may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, to host and protect its services. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to third-party pages take you outside this website. We do not control those pages or their data-handling practices.

8. Data Sharing

The current app does not send local progress to us or provide a developer-operated service for sharing it with other players.

Data processed by Google's advertising software is communicated to Google and may be handled by service providers or advertising participants as described in Google's information. We do not represent that advertising data remains solely on your device.

Information you send us by email is handled through the services needed to receive and answer your request. We may disclose information we control when required by applicable law or a valid legal process, or where necessary to address a security incident or protect legal rights.

9. Data Retention

Local game data: retained on your device until you clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to Android's operation and any actions you take.

Correspondence: retained for as long as reasonably needed to answer your enquiry, resolve the matter and meet applicable legal obligations. You may ask us to delete information we control, subject to any lawful reason to retain it.

Third-party data: retention is determined by the relevant provider and its policies. We cannot set or verify a single retention period for all information held by Google, GitHub or email providers.

10. Data Security

We use the platform's private app storage for local progress and limit access to information that you send us. Google states that data transmitted by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS.

No app, device, email channel or online service can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your device protected and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information in support messages.

11. Children’s Privacy

The Art of Dining is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. The current app has no registration or age-verification system.

If you believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact takodadigital@gmail.com. We will review the request and take appropriate steps for information under our control. Requests concerning information held by Google or another provider may also need to be addressed to that provider.

12. International Data Processing

The developer is based in Cyprus. Google, GitHub and email service providers operate internationally, so information they handle may be processed outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area, depending on the service.

Their privacy documentation describes the arrangements and safeguards they use. We do not claim that all processing occurs in Cyprus or that information never crosses national borders.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and the circumstances, applicable law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive certain information in a portable form, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a data-protection authority.

Send requests about information we control to takodadigital@gmail.com. We may need proportionate information to understand the request and confirm that it relates to you. We will explain any limitation that applies rather than promise a particular outcome in every case.

Use Android settings to remove local app data. For information controlled by Google or another service, consult that provider's privacy controls and request channels. There is no account to close in this app.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data-handling practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the current policy. Please review it before using a new release, particularly if advertising or other online features change.

Publishing an updated policy does not replace any notice or consent required by applicable law.
